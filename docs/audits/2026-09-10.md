| Finding | Before                                                                                                                       | After                                                                                                                                         | Commit   |

Compound assignments now share the ordinary binary-operation generator rather
than maintaining a second arithmetic implementation. They evaluate their
target address and operands once. Explicit throws and exception propagation
also share the same transfer generator.

The tests cover signed and unsigned 32/64-bit division, narrow bitwise values,
zero divisors, signed overflow, narrowing conversions, array lvalue evaluation,
cross-function exception propagation, cleanup order, catch-all handlers,
fractional payloads, large floating values, NaNs, infinities, and negative zero.
New successful runtime cases include LLVM verification and run at O0 and O3;
runtime-failure cases check both optimization levels too.

Commit 6b63e9f2 updates two existing source-inspection tests to locate the shared
arithmetic helper independently of its visibility. Their performance and LLVM
assertions remain intact; all 88 codegen tests pass.
